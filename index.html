esoji

Find, keep and tidy the photos that matter. A Mac app on top of Apple Photos that searches in your own words, suggests what to heart and which albums to make, and clears out duplicates.

Download for Mac

Free · Apple silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 26 or later
The chat needs Claude Code, signed in with a paid Claude plan. Everything else works without it.

Search in your own words

“The dog on a summit”, “sunsets from the van”. esoji understands what's in each photo, not just its date.

Suggested Hearts

Your best recent photos you haven't hearted yet, picked to match your taste.

Suggested Albums

Trips and moments gathered into albums, ready to create with one click.

Duplicates

Near-identical shots grouped, the best one picked to keep, merged the way iPhone does.

Your photos never leave your Mac

Understanding your pictures, search, suggestions, duplicates and face matching all happen on your Mac. No account, no analytics.

esoji only changes your library when you click: it suggests, you press Apply, and deleting always asks first.

Getting started

  1. Download and open esoji.dmg.
  2. Drag esoji to Applications, then open it from there.
  3. Allow access to Photos when it asks. esoji downloads its search engine (about 3 GB) and reads your library in the background. Search gets better as it goes.
  4. Optional, for the chat: install Claude Code and sign in, then turn on Claude in esoji.
