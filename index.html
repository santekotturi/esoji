Getting started

  1. Download and open esoji.dmg.
  2. Drag esoji to Applications, then open it from there.
  3. Allow access to Photos when it asks. esoji downloads its search engine (about 3 GB) and reads your library in the background. Search gets better as it goes.
  4. Chat. With Apple Intelligence on, the chat works right away, on your Mac. Or install Claude Code or Codex, sign in, and turn it on in esoji's Settings.
  5. Optional: Morning esoji. Turn it on in Settings for a quiet note each morning with new photos worth a favorite, finished trips and duplicates.

Or choose none: there's no chat, and search, suggestions and duplicates all still work on your Mac.

On your Mac, always

Photo understanding (EmbeddingGemma 2, Apple Vision), search, Suggested Favorites and Albums, duplicates, face matching and Morning esoji all run locally. The index lives in your user Library folder, and nothing is uploaded.

Apple Intelligence, on your Mac

On a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on, Apple's on-device model reads your searches, refines them and runs the chat. What it gets stays on your Mac, so it needs no account and asks nothing first. Without it, search gives instant results.

Works offline

The first launch downloads the search engine and model once (about 3 GB). After that, search, suggestions, duplicates and the Apple Intelligence chat work with no internet. Exceptions: Claude Code or Codex if you choose them, and photos stored only in iCloud, which Photos has to download first.

What Claude Code or Codex can see

  • Nothing until you turn one on, in esoji's Settings
  • The words you type, with People names swapped for stand-ins like PERSON_A (unless you choose to share names)
  • Handles like S3.12 instead of photo ids
  • When a request needs it: the day, up to 2 places and 10 scene words per photo
  • Email addresses and phone numbers are stripped out

What they never see

Your photos or any thumbnail of them, image embeddings, face data, photo ids, file names or your library as a whole. Handles only mean something on your Mac, in that one conversation.

The assistant can't change anything

Whichever assistant you use, its tools run in a macOS sandbox with no network access. They can read esoji's index and write proposals, nothing else. Changes happen only when you press Apply, and deleting needs your click in a confirmation, then macOS asks again. A check on every build enforces this.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, analytics or telemetry. Besides the setup download, esoji checks for a new version at most once a day: one request for GitHub's public latest-release page, with an esoji/<version> User-Agent and nothing about you or your photos. You can turn it off in Settings → Updates.

Forget means forget

Hide a photo and everything esoji worked out from it is deleted. Settings → Privacy deletes your chats, or all of esoji's data.

The limits

Stand-in names are pseudonyms, not anonymity: Claude or Codex can tell the same person appears in several photos. What Anthropic or OpenAI keeps depends on your account's privacy settings. Apple Intelligence's model is small: it handles short requests well, while Claude Code or Codex do better with long, many-step ones.
